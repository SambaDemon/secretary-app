Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21.

Secretary is a private application used by a single person, its owner (Oleksandr Sukhariev). It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent it accesses the owner's Gmail mailbox and Google Calendar to read messages and attachments, to prepare drafts and to propose calendar events.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide these functions to the owner. It is processed on the owner's own computer, and parts of it may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has chosen in order to generate replies. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete all stored data by deleting the application's local data.
