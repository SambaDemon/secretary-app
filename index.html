Secretary

Secretary is a private, single-user AI assistant that works for its owner, Oleksandr Sukhariev. It reads the owner's incoming email, drafts replies, and proposes calendar events through the Gmail and Google Calendar APIs.

It never sends an email or creates an event on its own: every action is shown to the owner and runs only after he approves it.

The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.
